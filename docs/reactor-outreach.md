# Reactor Outreach — Email Template

Shorter, more human, less AI-sounding. Keep it under 200 words.

---

Subject: A tool I built for your full-length reactions (I'm a patron)

Hi [Name],

I'm one of your Patreon subscribers — been following since [specific thing they reacted to]. You might recognize my name from a few comments.

I built a free desktop app called WatchAlong that syncs legally owned movie files with full-length Patreon reactions. One play button, no re-syncing, handles the 24fps drift. Open source, everything local, no accounts or tracking.

github.com/nizzyG/WatchAlong

I built it because I got tired of syncing manually. Figured your other patrons might find it useful too.

A few things:
- It's free. No catch.
- Still requires a Patreon subscription — doesn't bypass anything.
- If you have someone technical you trust, I'd welcome them reviewing the code.
- I'm not looking for sponsorship or partnership. Just sharing something I made.

If you think your community would be into it, I'd love to share it there. But your call entirely — wanted to check with you first.

Thanks for the content.
- [Your name]

---

## Customization per reactor

1. **The opener** — one specific reaction or moment. "Been following since your Blade Runner reaction" or "Your Severance recaps are consistently my favorite."
2. **If there's history** — "You replied to my comment about [thing]" or "I'm the guy who requested [reaction]."
3. **Skip the Across the Universe story** unless they also do musicals.

## Sending strategy

- Tuesday-Thursday, 10-11 AM Eastern
- Stagger: send to 2, wait 5 business days, send to 2 more
- Track responses in a text file: name, date sent, outcome
- One link in the email: the GitHub repo. Let the README do the rest.
